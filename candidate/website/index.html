02 / HOW THE DESK WORKS

Three seats.
One shared risk picture.

Each process owns a distinct job. NATS carries orders, market events, fills and status updates between the desk and the simulated exchange.

01EXISTING · PYTHON
↗

Taker

Follows short-term mid-price movement and crosses the spread with fill-and-kill orders. Its position and mark-to-market status are published once per second.

Quoter

Posts conservative two-sided limit quotes. It tracks fills, caps local inventory and throttles quote refreshes as the market moves.

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Built by testing
the assumptions.

The exchange protocol documentation was incomplete in places. Small, reproducible probes helped establish how fills and replies behaved before those assumptions were used in the desk.

Open development notes ↗
A

Reply volume matters

A Y 0 reply means accepted but unfilled. The hedger updates its position only by volume actually executed.

B

Fast fills need fast signals

One-second status snapshots were too slow for risk control, so immediate fill events are also published across the desk.

C

Risk control has a cost

The sample run recorded 199 zero-fill hedge replies and 478 rejected cancels. A future iteration could add thresholding, cooldown and retry backoff.
